I led this industry-academic collaboration project, developing a smart home system that dynamically adjusts music and lighting to enhance the living experience.

- Developed a framework that aligns music, emotion, and light into a unified ambient experience.
- Implemented a working demo using the Philips Hue system to validate the approach in real environments.
